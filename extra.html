Extras: Drinks, Desserts & More

Not every food adventure has to be a full meal! This page is dedicated to the little extras, from sweet treats and refreshing drinks to snacks that are worth trying. These reviews focus on taste, price, and whether each treat is worth it.

Drinks Worth Trying: Buffables

My Takes

Buffables is a fun spot to grab a refreshing drink when you're craving something sweet! I love the idea of being able to switch up my usual drink and order a variety of different options. It is a great option when you want a little treat during the day, and I'd definitely recommend checking it out if you're looking for something fun to sip on!

What I Tried

I got the Guava & Go Refresher!

Price & Value

My total came to about $7. This drink was extremely refreshing and fun to grab with friends every once in a while. I would say it is about the price you'll pay for any drink nowadays, but the menu in itself is a bit on the pricier side!

My Rating

★★★★☆

Desserts Worth Trying: Novel Ice Cream

My Takes

Novel Ice Cream is such a fun spot when you're craving something sweet! I love trying new ice cream flavors, and it's a great place to stop for a sweet treat with friends. Whether you're looking for something after dinner or just want to satisfy your sweet tooth, it's a cute place to check out. I'd definitely recommend it if you're in the mood for ice cream!

What I Tried

I ordered the Dough Melt with Butter Pecan ice cream!

Price & Value

The price for a Dough Melt - which is a glazed donut stuffed with ice cream - is $7.50. This is totally worth it and so yummy! Their ice cream is so creamy and the dessert is filling and completely shareable.

My Rating

★★★★★

Snacks Worth Trying: Laymoon Cafe

My Takes

Laymoon Cafe is such a cute spot to visit when you're looking for a refreshing drink and a little pick-me-up snack! I love trying cafes with unique drink options, and it's a fun place to meet up with friends or take a break from a busy day. It's definitely worth checking out if you're looking for a new cafe to add to your list!

What I Tried

I got the Banana Date Latte and a Zaatar Bagel!

Price & Value

The total came to around $20! This is a bit on the pricier side, but the bagel was extremely filling and the coffee is authentic. The atmosphere is very welcoming and comfortable, this is one of my favorite places to just sit and study!

My Rating

★★★★★
