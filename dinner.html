Dinner Reviews

Whether you're planning a dinner with friends or just looking for a new, affordable place to try, these reviews focus on food quality, portions, atmosphere, and whether the experience is worth the money.

Harumi Sushi & Sake

My Takes

Harumi Sushi & Sake is such a great spot if you're craving sushi! I love that they have a variety of rolls to choose from, and the food makes for a fun dinner out with friends. The atmosphere is perfect for a casual night out and even if you want to dress up, and the food is guaranteed fresh. I will 100% be coming back to this place when I am craving sushi!

What I Ordered

I ordered the Oasis and Super Crunch Shrimp specialty rolls!

Price & Value

The total for both rolls came out to $27. This is definitely on the pricier side since I would still want to order an appetizer and potentially drinks. However, I definitely think it is worth it considering the quality and freshness of the fish.

My Rating

★★★★★

Taco Boy's

My Takes

Taco Boy's is such a great spot if you're craving Mexican food! I love having a casual place to go when I want something satisfying without making dinner a whole event. It is a fun option for grabbing a bite with friends, and I'd definitely come back when I'm craving tacos and/or Mexican food!

What I Ordered

I ordered three tacos; Carne Asada, El Pastor, and Pollo!

Price & Value

The price per taco is $3.50, so for three it was around $10! This is super affordable and the food is extremely filling. There is a salad bar as well, so you are able to dress up your tacos with however many toppings and salsas you'd like.

My Rating

★★★★★

Industry Standard

My Takes

Industry Standard is such a cute spot for a dinner out! I love the atmosphere because it feels a little more elevated while still being a fun place to enjoy a meal with friends. It's a great option when you want to try something different from your usual restaurants, and I would definitely recommend checking it out for a fun night out!

What I Ordered

I ordered the Girl Dinner; a drink of choice, a "bump" of caviar, caesar salad, and vodka pasta!

Price & Value

The price of the girl dinner is $25! This is extremely worth it since the meal combo includes so much, including drinks. However, I do wish there was more variety in what could be included in the meal.

My Rating

★★★★☆
