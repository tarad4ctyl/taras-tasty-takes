Lunch Reviews

Whether you're looking for a quick lunch between your classes or a place to sit down and enjoy a meal with friends, these reviews focus on taste, portions, price, and overall value.

Amici Pizza

My Takes

Amici Pizza is one of those places that's perfect when you're craving a good, comforting meal. The pizza was flavorful, the crust was fluffy, and the portions were satisfying. I also liked that there were plenty of different options to choose from, so it's easy to find something you'll like. It's a great casual spot to grab food with friends, and I'd definitely go back when I'm in the mood for Italian food!

What I Ordered

I ordered pizza (1/2 Caesar, 1/2 Margherita)!

Price & Value

My meal cost approximately $25. I split my meal with a friend so it was around $13 per. I believe this is a great price since the food is so filling!

My Rating

★★★★☆

Maple House

My Takes

Maple House was such a fun place to stop for lunch! I really enjoyed the food, especially because everything was flavorful and filling without feeling too heavy. The menu could have had more variety, but the atmosphere made it a nice place to sit and take your time. I'd definitely come back to Maple House, especially when I'm in the mood to chat with my friends!

What I Ordered

I got the tomato flatbread with pepperoni and warm brie to split!

Price & Value

My meal cost approximately $20 for the flatbread and appetizer. I would say this is definitely a bang for your buck! The flatbread is filling, but light, and the brie felt like a nice addition of flavor.

My Rating

★★★★★

Lydia's Kitchen & Market

My Takes

Lydia's Kitchen & Market has such a cozy and welcoming feel to it! I really enjoyed trying the food because everything tasted fresh and flavorful. I also liked that it felt a little different from a typical restaurant since it is surrounded by different shops, which made the experience more memorable. It's a great place to grab a meal and explore something new, and I'd definitely go back!

What I Ordered

I ordered the BLT Sandwich with rosemary fries!

Price & Value

My meal was approximately $16. I would say this is a decent price for a sandwich, but this would not be my first option if I was looking for an affordable and filling meal. This is an extremely cute place to go with friends though and part of the value is in the atmosphere.

My Rating

★★★★☆
